Privacy Policy

Taseeriya Calendar

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taseeriya Calendar ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

1. Information We Access

The app may request access to your device's location to provide location-based features such as Qibla direction and prayer timings. Location permission is requested only when these features require it.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the app may be used to:

  • Calculate Qibla direction.
  • Provide location-based prayer timings.
  • Save your Tasbeeh counter progress on your device.
  • Display calendar dates and Islamic events.
  • Provide the app's PDF and other features.

3. Data Storage

Certain app preferences and Tasbeeh counter information may be stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account registration system in the app.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The app may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers and usage information, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more information, please visit Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Location Permissions

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may affect Qibla direction and location-based prayer timing features.

6. Children's Privacy

Taseeriya Calendar is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
